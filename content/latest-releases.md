# Latest releases

Tibor is a British-Hungarian sound artist and experimental musician based on the Isle of Man. His work is rooted in deep listening and in the idea that sound can uncover aspects of a place that remain hidden from sight. Architecture, landscape, memory and the conditions of a performance are not treated as backgrounds to the music, but as active collaborators in its making.

Across these three projects, sound becomes a way of paying attention: to what a place holds, to how a room shapes an encounter, and to the traces that remain when different times and materials are allowed to coexist.

---

## Agneash

*Agneash* begins with a place, but it is not an attempt to describe that place from a distance. It approaches location through attention: listening for atmosphere, memory and the less visible forces that give an environment its character.

The music unfolds as a form of sonic archaeology. Tones gather, shift and recede; details appear at the edge of perception; space is felt through resonance rather than mapped through image. What emerges is not a fixed portrait, but an encounter between an environment and the act of listening to it.

Within Tibor's wider practice, *Agneash* reflects an ongoing interest in how sound can hold both personal memory and shared place. It asks what becomes audible when composition begins not with a statement, but with sustained attention.

---

## Boxes (Live)

*Boxes (Live)* treats performance as a meeting between sound, body and room. The title suggests containers and boundaries, but the music tests how permeable those boundaries really are: between instrument and environment, composed material and improvisation, performer and listener.

Rather than presenting the live setting as a neutral stage, the work allows its architecture, acoustics and atmosphere to become part of the piece. Rhythmic and textured sound is shaped in real time, making the performance responsive to the space in which it happens. The room does not simply receive the music; it changes it.

The recording preserves a trace of that exchange rather than a definitive version. In this sense, *Boxes (Live)* connects closely to Tibor's practice of treating place as an active collaborator and listening as a creative act shared with an audience.

---

## Layers

*Layers* considers how sounds, memories and moments accumulate without fully replacing one another. Each new element alters what is already present: a rhythm changes the meaning of a sustained tone; a voice brings the human body into an abstract space; silence reveals details that density had concealed.

The work develops through overlap and gradual transformation. Its materials can be heard as surfaces, but also as histories: different times and places remaining active within the same field of sound. Listening becomes an act of moving between them, noticing not only the individual parts but the relationships that form at their edges.

As part of Tibor's wider practice, *Layers* draws attention to sound as something spatial and remembered. It invites the listener to stay with complexity long enough for its quieter structures to emerge.
