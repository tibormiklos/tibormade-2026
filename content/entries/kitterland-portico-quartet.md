On 13 September 2025 Kitterland opened for Portico Quartet at the Kings Court Theatre, King William's College, Castletown. It was the band's only live date in the British Isles that year, presented by Waves of Wonder with support from the Isle of Man Arts Council, with Soundology also on the bill.

Kitterland is Tibor Miklós and Joff Whitten, working with filmmaker Anthony Dalton: live, improvised cinematic music made with analogue synths, looping pedals, guitars and voice.
