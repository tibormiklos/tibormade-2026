On 13 September 2025 Kitterland opened for Portico Quartet at the Kings Court Theatre at King William's College in Castletown, the band's only date in the British Isles that year. Waves of Wonder put the night on with support from the Isle of Man Arts Council, and Soundology was on the bill too.

Kitterland is Tibor and Joff Whitten, with filmmaker Anthony Dalton: improvised music for film, made live with analogue synths, loop pedals, guitars and voice.
