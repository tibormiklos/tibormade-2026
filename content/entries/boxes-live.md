*Boxes* is Tibor's live set: rhythm and texture played on electronics, never quite the same twice because the room gets a say in it. The acoustics, the size of the space and the mood of the people in it change what comes out, so each performance is built for the night rather than brought in finished.

This recording is one of those nights, kept as it happened rather than tidied up afterwards. It is closer to a document than a definitive version, and that is the point: the room is on the record as much as the music is.
