*Agneash* is the first work in the **Organism** series: site-specific compositions that explore architecture as a living instrument and places as collaborators.

Created for the Methodist Chapel in Agneash on the Isle of Man, the work began with listening. Every sound was gathered from within the building itself: the harmonium, the wooden pews, footsteps, air, and silence. Alongside these recordings, the chapel's unique acoustic signature—its impulse response—was captured, allowing the space to shape every sound that followed.

In the studio, these fragments were transformed, layered, and returned to the acoustic character of the chapel. The resulting seven movements unfold slowly, inviting the listener to experience the building not simply as a setting, but as an active presence within the music.

Originally presented inside the chapel itself, *Agneash* is an invitation to deep listening—to the subtle resonances that emerge when architecture, memory, and sound are allowed to inhabit one another.

The accompanying impulse response of Agneash Methodist Chapel is included with this release, allowing other musicians, composers, and sound artists to place their own work inside the same extraordinary acoustic space.

Composition, field recordings, harmonium, processing, production and artwork: **Tibor Miklós**
Recorded at Agneash Methodist Chapel, Isle of Man. Originally presented in August 2025.
