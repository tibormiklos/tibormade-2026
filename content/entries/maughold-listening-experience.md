*Layers* (Organism II) is a site-specific composition created for St Maughold's Church on the Isle of Man as part of Maughold Week 2026.

The work began with listening.

Over a number of weeks, recordings were gathered within and around the church: wind moving through the churchyard, birdsong, stone, wood, the organ, and the human voice. Alongside these sounds, the church's own impulse response—its unique acoustic fingerprint—was captured, allowing the building itself to become an active collaborator in the composition.

In the studio, these fragments were transformed, layered, and returned to the space from which they came. Every sound heard in the finished work carries something of the church within it, shaped not only by the materials that were recorded there, but by the building's own resonance.

The title *Layers* reflects the many strata held within this remarkable place: landscape and architecture, history and memory, sound and silence. Rather than telling a story, the piece invites a slower way of listening, allowing these layers to emerge gradually over time.

Originally conceived as an installation, *Layers* was presented continuously within St Maughold's Church throughout Maughold Week, alongside a series of daily listening gatherings. This recording is presented in the same spirit: as an uninterrupted, circular work with no fixed beginning or end, inviting listeners to enter and leave wherever they choose.

The accompanying impulse response of St Maughold's Church is included with this release, allowing other musicians, composers, and sound artists to place their own work inside the same extraordinary acoustic space.

Composition, field recordings, organ, processing, production and artwork: **Tibor Miklós**
Vocal improvisation: **Lauren Gray**
Recorded at St Maughold's Church and the surrounding landscape, Isle of Man. Created for Maughold Week 2026.
